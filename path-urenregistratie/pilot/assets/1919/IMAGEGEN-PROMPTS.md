## `hero.jpg`

> Create a brand-new photorealistic editorial landscape photograph to be used as the hero image of a premium Dutch employee timesheet dashboard. Use the attached full-page UI mockup only as a composition, lighting, and color reference; output only the landscape photo, with no UI, no text, no logos, no borders, and no watermark. Scene: a quiet winding pale sand or shell path through Dutch coastal dunes at sunrise, path entering from the lower right and curving toward the middle distance and warm low sun; North Sea and hazy horizon subtly visible on the left; layered marram grass, heather, dark green dune scrub, soft cream sky, scattered clouds catching gold light. Sophisticated natural photography, realistic depth, slightly cinematic but credible, restrained warm cream/gold with deep green shadows. Compose for a 4:3 landscape crop and ensure useful detail from edge to edge, especially the right side; no people, vehicles, buildings, signs, or animals. Aim for at least 1536 x 1152 pixels.

Uitvoer: `pilot/assets/1919/hero.jpg` — 1448 × 1086, 276.181 bytes.

## `document.jpg`

> Create a brand-new photorealistic editorial still-life photograph for the lower section of a premium Dutch employee timesheet dashboard. Use the attached full-page UI mockup only as a composition, lighting, and color reference; output only the still-life photo, with no UI chrome, no borders, no watermark, and no recognizable brand logo. Landscape 3:2 composition, at least 1500 x 1000 pixels. A single off-white printed customer timesheet lies diagonally on a deep charcoal-navy textured desk, tilted about 7 degrees counterclockwise. The paper should clearly read visually as a polished business timesheet through subtle tables, ruled rows, tiny neutral headings and a restrained green accent mark, but all small text must be generic or too soft to identify and must contain no personal information. Soft window light from upper left, premium tactile paper, believable shadows. A leafy dark-green houseplant enters from the lower-right edge, slightly out of focus, matching the reference composition. Keep generous dark negative space around the sheet so it crops well in a web banner. No hands, laptop, pen, coffee, people, logos, legible names, or watermarks.

Uitvoer: `pilot/assets/1919/document.jpg` — 1536 × 1024, 189.784 bytes.
